# Zeus brand and visual assets

Zeus is a separate identity from Coucou. This repository/package intentionally does **not** redistribute the Coucou name, Mochi character, Coucou/Mochi icons, Coucou sounds, demo GIFs, videos, or other artwork reserved by the upstream author.

The black-dog Zeus mascot, icons, vector base, application UI and animation specification in `shared/branding/` and `apps/mobile/assets/rive/` were created for Zeus. Before a public/commercial release, replace this paragraph with the exact legal rights holder and final asset-license terms desired by the Zeus owner.

Do not import upstream protected visual/audio assets into a Zeus release unless you separately obtain permission from their rights holder.
